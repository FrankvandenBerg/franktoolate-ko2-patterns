K.O. II patterns

not connected
Connect the K.O. II over USB and click Connect. Chrome will ask for MIDI permission.

Reading is read-only: the projects are requested over MIDI and nothing on the sampler changes until you choose to write.

Patterns are numbered 01–99 per group (A–D), as in the Teenage Engineering guide. "b" is the pattern length in bars (1 bar = 384 ticks at 96 ppqn), "n" the number of notes. Pads are named by the labels printed on them (7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3 / . 0 ENTER). Left/right arrows switch scene (also while a scene plays), keys 1–9 switch project, space plays or stops the selected scene. Moving: drag a pattern onto another pattern to swap them, or onto a group to move it there; within a group, scenes follow along. Deleting a pattern leaves its group silent in scenes that used it; clearing a scene also removes it from the song. Playback sends MIDI notes on channel 1 (group A = notes 36–47, B = 48–59, C = 60–71, D = 72–83, in the order . 0 ENTER 1–9); do not leave the K.O. II in record mode, or the notes will be recorded. Pads in keys mode play at their root note.
