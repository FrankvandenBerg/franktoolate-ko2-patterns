K.O. II patterns

not connected
Connect the K.O. II over USB and click Connect. Chrome will ask for MIDI permission.

Reading is read-only: the projects are requested over MIDI and nothing on the sampler changes until you choose to write.

Patterns are numbered 01–99 per group (A–D), as in the Teenage Engineering guide. "b" is the pattern length in bars (1 bar = 384 ticks at 96 ppqn), "n" the number of notes. Pad numbers 1–12 are the internal order, not the labels printed on the pads. Left/right arrows switch pattern, keys 1–9 switch project, space plays or stops. Moving: drag a pattern onto another pattern to swap them, or onto a group to move it there; within a group, scenes follow along. Playback sends MIDI notes to the sampler (group A = notes 36–47, B = 48–59, C = 60–71, D = 72–83); do not leave the K.O. II in record mode, or the notes will be recorded. Pads in keys mode play at their root note.
